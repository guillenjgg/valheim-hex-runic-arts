# HexRunicArts

Discover the forgotten runic arts of Valheim. Uncover ancient lore, complete quests, and unlock powerful abilities inspired by the gods of Norse mythology.

* Currently there is only one ability, which is called Splintering. More to come!

## Features

- **Runic Quests** – Discover ancient runestones and complete quests to unlock abilities.
- **Splintering** – Channel Thor's thunder through your axe to shatter nearby logs and stumps into wood.
- **Quest Tracker** – Track your progress with an in-game overlay.

## Installation

1. Install BepInEx for Valheim.
2. Extract the mod into your Valheim `BepInEx/plugins` folder.
3. Launch the game.
